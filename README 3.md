# DTI Ω × HedgeOps Ω

**A proposed curriculum for temporal intelligence, economic capacity, and reality-convergent decision systems.**

> Status: Conceptual research and curriculum proposal. The framework, metrics, and educational credentials described here are not presented as independently validated, accredited, or endorsed by any university.

## Overview

DTI Ω × HedgeOps Ω integrates temporal-capacity measurement with uncertainty-aware economic decisions, causal inference, multi-agent intelligence, systemic risk, human constraints, and empirical feedback.

**Learning progression:** Time → Capacity → State → Uncertainty → Opportunity → Decision → Risk → Action → Reality → Learning.

The central research question is:

> Can jointly modeling temporal capacity and environmental uncertainty produce better economic decisions than existing approaches?

## Central constructs

- **Dual-state representation:** `Z_t = (M_t, Θ_t)`, with `M_t` representing environmental state and `Θ_t` temporal-capacity state.
- **Temporal Option Capacity (TOC):** proposed measurement construct requiring operationalization and validation.
- **Temporal Fragmentation Index (TFI):** proposed context-dependent measure of fragmentation.
- **Temporal-Economic Opportunity Value (TODV):** proposed expected-value construct conditioned on opportunity, capacity, risk, and uncertainty.
- **Reality convergence:** comparing predictions with observed outcomes to update decisions.

These are *proposed constructs*, not established performance claims. Definitions, units, identification assumptions, and measurement procedures remain research tasks.

## Research and validation requirements

1. Specify operational definitions for TOC, TEV, TODV, TFI, autonomy, slack, and systemic risk.
2. Compare against at least three independent baseline approaches.
3. Conduct ablation tests to identify which components add value.
4. Evaluate counterfactual claims using appropriate causal designs.
5. Measure prediction-versus-reality divergence and calibration.
6. Document failures, uncertainty, privacy, fairness, autonomy, recovery, and well-being constraints.
7. Release reproducible code, datasets where permitted, protocols, and results when available.
