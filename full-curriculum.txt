THE Ω DOCTRINE
The entire curriculum can be compressed into ten principles:
Time is a state variable.
Capacity is not equivalent to available time.
Every discretionary second creates an option.
Options have economic value.
Opportunity exists only relative to execution capacity.
Every decision is a hypothesis about reality.
Prediction is not causation.
Risk is structural, not merely statistical.
Reality must arbitrate models.
Optimization must preserve human agency.
And the ultimate curriculum equation is:
\boxed{ \textbf{ Temporal Capacity \times Epistemic Intelligence \times Causal Decision Quality \times Risk Control \times Execution Feasibility } }
subject to:
\boxed{ Autonomy,\ Recovery,\ WellBeing,\ Equity }
Final educational objective
Graduate people capable of engineering systems that convert scarce temporal capacity into higher-quality economic options, make decisions under uncertainty, execute those decisions under constraints, and continuously converge toward reality through empirical feedback.
That is the DTI Ω × HedgeOps Ω discipline.
