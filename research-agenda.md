# Research agenda

## Main hypothesis

Joint modeling of temporal capacity and environmental uncertainty can improve decision quality relative to approaches that model either dimension alone.

## Questions for reviewers

1. Which constructs are distinct from established concepts in operations research, time-use research, decision theory, and economics?
2. How should TOC, TFI, TEV, and TODV be operationally defined and validated?
3. What baseline models are appropriate for different application domains?
4. Which datasets or pilot interventions could establish predictive and causal validity?
5. When does the additional model complexity fail to improve outcomes?
6. How can autonomy, recovery, well-being, and equity be preserved?

## Suggested first experiment

Choose one bounded scheduling or resource-allocation problem. Pre-register the outcome, constraints, and comparison methods. Compare a simple heuristic, standard optimization, uncertainty-aware optimization, and the integrated temporal-capacity approach. Report confidence intervals, robustness checks, ablations, and negative results.

## Evidence status

The supplied curriculum outlines proposed constructs and experiments but does not provide empirical results, source citations, executable implementations, or institutional approval. Those are next-stage deliverables.
